# Apple Watch

The watch app shows your items and lets you add new ones from your wrist. It keeps its own copy of your items and syncs it through iCloud, so it works when your phone is out of reach.

## What the watch shows

- The Items list, newest first. Tap an item to read its notes.
- Add, at the top of the list, to dictate or write a new item's title.
- Swipe left on an item to delete it.

## The complication

The complication shows how many items you have on the watch face. It updates when the watch app saves a change, or when a change from another device arrives. Tap it to open the app.

## How the watch syncs

The watch syncs with iCloud on its own, the same way the phone does. It does not need the phone nearby, only a connection: Wi-Fi, a cellular watch plan, or the phone's connection when the two are close. A change you make on the watch reaches the phone through iCloud, and a change on the phone reaches the watch the same way.

The watch fetches changes when you open the app. A change made elsewhere can take a few minutes to reach a watch whose app has not been opened.
