# Items

An item is one thing you write down: a title, and notes if you want them. Scaffold keeps your items on your device and syncs them to your other devices through your own iCloud account. This article is a placeholder for the app's first real feature.

## What an item holds

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| Title | One line that names the item. An item needs a title to be saved. |
| Notes | Any further text. Notes are optional. |
| Created | The day you added the item. The app sets it, and it never changes. |

## Adding and changing an item

1. Tap the plus button on the Items list.
2. Type a title, and notes if you want them.
3. Tap Save.

To change an item, open it and tap Edit. Your change is saved on the device at once, and sent to your other devices the next time they sync.

## Deleting an item

Swipe left on an item in the list and tap Delete, or open the item and tap Delete at the bottom. A deletion reaches every device that syncs with this one. The item cannot be restored after that.

## On iPad

On iPad the Items list and the open item sit side by side. Tap an item in the list to show it on the right. When the app shares the screen with another app, it uses the iPhone layout.

## In this app

- The Items tab, on iPhone and iPad.
- The Scaffold widget on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen, which shows how many items you have and the newest ones.
- The watch app and its complication.
